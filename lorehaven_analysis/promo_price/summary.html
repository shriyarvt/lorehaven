Discount price estimates

Sample is customers who were offered a promo. The dollar on that offer is the randomized price. The two periods are estimated and shown separately, because the grid is $10 to $14 through winter 2019 and $7 to $11 from spring 2019 on. Each period uses a linear probability model with a dummy for each dollar, region fixed effects, and year-season fixed effects. Standard errors are clustered by customer. The dashed line is the raw average. Filled circles hold region and year-season constant, with 95% intervals. One-season revenue per person offered is the membership rate times the price.

Through winter 2019

$11 and $10 are tied on revenue per person offered, at $3.884 and $3.881. The $0.003 gap has a clustered standard error of $0.015. Each dollar covers about 208,782 customer-seasons. The membership rate falls from 38.8% at $10 to 25.1% at $14. For the same customer, membership at $10 is 12.3 percentage points higher than at $14 (standard error 0.12 percentage points).

Among people who were not members last season, the sign-up rate falls from 17.3% at $10 to 5.3% at $14. Sign-up revenue per person at risk is highest at $10 ($1.73). $11 is next, $0.25 lower, and that gap is 16.9 clustered standard errors.

Among people who were members last season, the churn rate rises from 11.6% at $10 to 29.0% at $14.

Holding region and year-season constant changes every rate by 0.05 percentage points. The logit differs from the linear probability model by less than 0.1 percentage points.

Spring 2019 on

Revenue per person offered is highest at $11 ($5.95). $10 is next, $0.25 lower, and that gap is 10.4 clustered standard errors. Each dollar covers about 94,134 customer-seasons. The membership rate falls from 65.4% at $7 to 54.1% at $11. For the same customer, membership at $7 is 7.2 percentage points higher than at $11 (standard error 0.17 percentage points).

Among people who were not members last season, the sign-up rate falls from 25.2% at $7 to 8.4% at $11. Sign-up revenue per person at risk is highest at $7 ($1.77). $8 is next, $0.13 lower, and that gap is 6.1 clustered standard errors.

Among people who were members last season, the churn rate rises from 5.1% at $7 to 12.1% at $11.

Holding region and year-season constant changes every rate by less than 0.1 percentage points. The logit differs from the linear probability model by less than 0.1 percentage points.
